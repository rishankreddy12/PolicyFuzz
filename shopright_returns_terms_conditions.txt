1. Returns Eligibility
1.1 Customers may request a return for eligible products within 30 days of the delivery date.
1.2 Products must generally be unused and in a resalable condition.
1.3 Customers must provide valid proof of purchase when requesting a return.
1.4 A return may be refused where the product has been significantly used or damaged after delivery.

2. Return Period
2.1 The return period begins on the date shown by the carrier as the delivery date.
2.2 Returns submitted on the last day of the return period may be accepted if the request is made before the end of that day.
2.3 Where an order contains multiple products delivered on different dates, each product will normally have its own return period.
2.4 Customers who are unable to submit a return request because of a temporary website issue should contact customer support as soon as possible.

3. Product Condition
3.1 Returned products should be in substantially the same condition as when they were delivered.
3.2 Minor signs of inspection or testing do not automatically make a product ineligible for return.
3.3 Products that have been used beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect them may be refused.
3.4 Products with scratches, dents, stains, missing components, or other physical damage may be subject to rejection or a reduced refund.
3.5 The determination of whether a product remains in acceptable condition will be made during inspection.

4. Packaging and Accessories
4.1 Customers should return products using the original packaging whenever reasonably possible.
4.2 Missing manuals, accessories, cables, or other components may result in a partial refund.
4.3 Minor damage to the original packaging alone will not normally prevent a return.
4.4 Products returned without their original packaging may still be accepted where the product itself is in acceptable condition.

5. Refunds
5.1 Approved returns will normally be refunded to the original payment method.
5.2 Refunds are processed after the returned product has been received and inspected.
5.3 Refund processing may take several business days after approval.
5.4 Where only part of an order is returned, the refund will normally apply only to the returned products.
5.5 ShopRight may deduct an appropriate amount where the condition of the returned product has materially reduced its value.

6.1 Customers are generally responsible for the cost of returning a product when returning it because of a change of mind.
6.2 Return shipping costs may be waived where the wrong product was delivered or the product arrived damaged.

6.4 ShopRight may reimburse return shipping where required by applicable law or where customer support has approved such reimbursement.

7.1 Products identified as non-returnable at the time of purchase cannot normally be returned.

9.1 Products purchased using a promotional discount remain subject to these return conditions unless the promotion states otherwise.
9.2 Refunds for discounted products will normally be based on the amount actually paid by the customer.
9.3 Promotional discount codes may not be reinstated after a return has been processed.
9.4 Where a promotional gift was provided with a qualifying purchase, ShopRight may require the gift to be returned when the qualifying product is returned.
9.5 Where only part of a promotional bundle is returned, ShopRight may adjust the refund to account for the promotion.

10.2 Exchanges are subject to the same product-condition requirements as returns.
10.3 Where the requested replacement is unavailable, ShopRight may issue a refund instead.
10.4 A replacement product will normally be shipped after the original product has been received or the exchange has otherwise been approved.
